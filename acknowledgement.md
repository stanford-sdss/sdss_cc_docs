Researchers who would like to acknowledge the compute, data, or research support provided by the SDSS Center for Computation may use the following templates, based on the type of support received:

# Compute Resources

We extend our gratitude to Stanford University and the Center for Computation at the Stanford Doerr School of Sustainability for providing the computational resources and support that were essential to our research outcomes.


# Data

We would like to express our appreciation to the Center for Computation at the Stanford Doerr School of Sustainability for the data resources and storage infrastructure that significantly contributed to our research findings.

# Research Support

We sincerely thank the Center for Computation at the Stanford Doerr School of Sustainability for their invaluable support and consultation in research computing, which played a key role in our research achievements.
