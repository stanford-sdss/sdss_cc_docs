# Compute

SDSS provides a range of on-premises and cloud-based computational resources to support the research needs of the Stanford Doerr School of Sustainability community.

::::{grid} 1 1 3 3

:::{grid-item-card} Sherlock
:link: sherlock

Dedicated CPUs and GPUs in Stanford's Sherlock cluster, accessible via the `serc` partition.
:::

:::{grid-item-card} Cloud Resources
:link: cloud-resources

Cloud compute that augments our on-premise cluster for flexible, scalable workloads.
:::

:::{grid-item-card} National Lab Resources
:link: national-lab-resources

Access to NSF ACCESS and other national HPC systems, with support from our Campus Champion.
:::

::::
