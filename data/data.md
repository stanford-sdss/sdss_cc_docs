# Data

SDSS provides storage and repository solutions to help researchers manage, archive, and publish their data throughout the research lifecycle.

::::{grid} 1 1 2 4

:::{grid-item-card} Oak
:link: oak

High-performance storage tied to Sherlock.
:::

:::{grid-item-card} Elm
:link: elm

Tape-based archival object store for long-term data needs.
:::

:::{grid-item-card} Stanford Digital Repository
:link: stanford-digital-repository

Long term storage for published research outputs, managed by Stanford Libraries.
:::

:::{grid-item-card} Redivis
:link: redivis

Research data platform with version control, DOIs, and reproducibility tools.
:::
