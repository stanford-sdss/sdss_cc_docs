# Oak

[Oak](https://docs.oak.stanford.edu/) is Stanford's high-performance computing storage system, closely tied to Sherlock and managed by the Stanford Research Computing team. SDSS offers PIs a standard **10TB Oak allocation at no charge**, with increased allocations available upon request. We also provide **free backups** of all Oak spaces to Google Cloud.

## Requesting an Allocation

Please email [sdss-compute@stanford.edu](mailto:sdss-compute@stanford.edu) to request a new Oak space.

## Managing Your Oak Space

### Access

Access to Oak spaces are managed by [Stanford workgroups](https://workgroup.stanford.edu/).  You can find the workgroup associated with your Oak space as `oak:<pi_sunet>`.  If you need any help with access administraiton, please reach out to [sdss-compute@stanford.edu](mailto:sdss-compute@stanford.edu).

### Files and Quotas

Every Oak space comes with a dashboard that provides information on storage quota, storage utiliziation, inodes, and per-user breakdowns.  Anyone who has access to the Oak space should have access to the dashboard.

To access your dashboard, [please see instructions on this page](https://docs.oak.stanford.edu/user-guide/dashboards/#accessing-your-dashboard).
