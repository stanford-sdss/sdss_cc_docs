# Redivis

[Redivis](https://redivis.com/Stanford) is a research data platform that combines data, metadata, and compute in one place. It provides DOIs, version control, and reproducibility features that commercial platforms can't match.

Browse [SDSS datasets on Redivis](https://redivis.com/Stanford) or contact [sdss-compute@stanford.edu](mailto:sdss-compute@stanford.edu) with inquiries.

## Accessing SDSS Datasets

Many datasets hosted by SDSS on Redivis can be accessed simply by:

* becoming a member of the [SDSS Data Repository organization](https://sdss.redivis.com/SDSS_data_repository)
* Browsing [the Datasets page](https://sdss.redivis.com/SDSS_data_repository/datasets) to find the dataset you're looking for

## Publishing Your Data

Interested in publishing your data on Redivis?  Reach out to [sdss-compute@stanford.edu](mailto:sdss-compute@stanford.edu) for more info!
