# Welcome to the SDSS-CC Technical Documentation

Welcome to the official technical documentation portal for the **Stanford Doerr School of Sustainability Center for Computation (SDSS-CC)**.

Our mission is to advance research and scholarship by providing the SDSS community with access to high-end computing, robust data storage, and advanced technical support. Whether you are running complex simulations, managing petabytes of data, or training the next generation of machine learning models, we provide the platforms to support your research.

---

::::{grid} 1 1 2 2

:::{grid-item-card} 🚀 Getting Started
New to our resources? Start here to get your accounts set up and learn the basics of our high-performance computing (HPC) environments.

- **Eligibility & Access:** Learn who can use SDSS-CC resources and how to request an account.
- **Connecting to the Clusters:** Step-by-step guides for logging in via SSH and utilizing interactive compute nodes.
:::

:::{grid-item-card} 💻 Compute Resources
Explore our portfolio of computational resources tailored to align with your digital research needs. All systems are maintained in collaboration with the Stanford Research Computing Center (SRCC).

- **Sherlock HPC Cluster & SERC Partition:** Access public partitions (normal, gpu, dev, bigmem, owners) as well as the dedicated SDSS serc partition.
- **NSF ACCESS Assistance:** Get help requesting resources on NSF ACCESS clusters.
- **Cloud Compute Resources:** SDSS augments our on-premise cluster with cloud resources. Reach out at [sdss-compute@stanford.edu](mailto:sdss-compute@stanford.edu) or book a cloud consultation.
:::

:::{grid-item-card} 💾 Storage & Data Management
Effectively manage, transfer, and optimize your research data across local clusters and cloud storage.

- **Oak Storage:** Stanford's high-performance storage system closely tied to Sherlock, offering SDSS PIs a standard 10TB allocation at no charge (expandable upon request) and free backups to Google Cloud.
- **Large-Scale Data Transfer:** Best practices for moving massive datasets (up to 50TB+) using Globus, rsync, and rclone.
- **Elm Archive:** An innovative tape-based object store for archival data needs. Contact [sdss-compute@stanford.edu](mailto:sdss-compute@stanford.edu) for more information.
:::

:::{grid-item-card} 🛠️ Software, Code & Tools
Access a rich portfolio of software, programming environments, and visualization tools.

- **Software & Modules:** Loading environments on Sherlock and using Containers (Singularity/Docker).
- **Programming & Notebooks:** Setting up Python environments and running Jupyter Notebooks.
- **Data Formats & Optimization:** Working with HDF5, zarr, and optimizing I/O performance.
:::
