# SDSS-CC News Archive

## Intro to Research Computing for SDSS

*October 30, 2024 · Green 361*

SDSS-CC held a hands-on office-hours workshop covering research computing at the Doerr School of Sustainability, including Sherlock, Google Cloud Platform, data management, and attendee questions. The original event included food and an opportunity to meet the support team.

For current workshops, office hours, and announcements, contact [sdss-compute@stanford.edu](mailto:sdss-compute@stanford.edu) or visit [Get In Touch](get-in-touch).
